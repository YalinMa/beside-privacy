Beside 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 7 日

Beside 是一个 Markdown 速记工具，包括 Chrome 扩展和可选的桌面版。一句话：你的笔记只在你自己的电脑上，我们收不到。

我们不收集任何数据

Beside 没有服务器，也没有账号系统。不收集、不上传、不出售你的笔记、个人信息或使用情况，也没有统计和广告代码。

笔记存在哪里

  • 你选择的文件夹：笔记存成 .md 文件，图片存在同一文件夹的 assets/ 子文件夹里。
  • 浏览器本地存储：扩展在 IndexedDB 和 localStorage 里保存笔记缓存、未写入文件的草稿、文件夹的访问凭据和主题设置。这些数据不会离开你的浏览器。

如果你把文件夹放在 Google Drive、iCloud 等网盘里，这些文件由网盘服务同步，适用该服务自己的隐私政策。Beside 不参与，也接触不到。

扩展申请的权限

  • sidePanel（侧边栏）：在浏览器侧边栏里显示笔记。
  • nativeMessaging（与本机程序通信）：如果你安装了 Beside 桌面版，扩展通过随它安装的本机小助手读写你选择的文件夹，这样就不用反复授权，还能很快看到文件变化。小助手只在你的电脑上运行，不联网。没装桌面版时这个权限不起作用。

扩展不读取你正在浏览的网页内容，也不访问你的浏览记录。

会产生网络请求的情况

Beside 本身不联网。只有当你的笔记里引用了网络图片时，显示笔记才会从该图片所在的网站加载图片，这和普通网页加载图片一样。点击笔记里的链接会在浏览器中打开对应网站。

删除数据

删除文件夹里的文件即可删除笔记。卸载扩展会清除浏览器里的缓存。

联系方式

有问题请到 GitHub 留言。政策有变化时会更新本页和上面的日期。

Beside Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Beside is a Markdown note-taking tool made up of a Chrome extension and an optional desktop app. In short: your notes stay on your own computer, and we never receive them.

We collect no data

Beside has no servers and no accounts. It does not collect, upload or sell your notes, personal information or usage data, and it contains no analytics or advertising code.

Where your notes are stored

  • A folder you choose: notes are saved as .md files, and images go in an assets/ subfolder of the same folder.
  • Local browser storage: the extension keeps a note cache, unsaved drafts, the folder access handle and your theme in IndexedDB and localStorage. This data never leaves your browser.

If you put the folder in a cloud drive such as Google Drive or iCloud, that service syncs the files under its own privacy policy. Beside is not involved and has no access to them.

Permissions

  • sidePanel: shows your notes in the browser side panel.
  • nativeMessaging: if you install the Beside desktop app, the extension reads and writes your chosen folder through a small local helper installed with it, so you don't have to re-grant access repeatedly and file changes show up quickly. The helper runs only on your computer and does not use the network. Without the desktop app, this permission does nothing.

The extension does not read the pages you visit or access your browsing history.

When network requests happen

Beside itself does not use the network. If a note references an image on the web, displaying that note loads the image from its website, as any web page would. Clicking a link in a note opens that site in your browser.

Deleting your data

Delete the files in your folder to delete your notes. Uninstalling the extension clears its browser cache.
